# Global TV Hub v0.16.0 — playback repair and sports expansion (pre-release)

Global TV Hub is a static PWA for German, international and sports television. The approved premium dark dashboard is unchanged. Selecting a configured channel stays inside the app.

The v0.16 candidate repairs Tennis Channel International after its previously working Rakuten feed stopped sending the CORS header required by Chrome. Its replacement international FAST source started in about four seconds, sustained advancing video and decoded audio for 15 seconds, and showed actual tennis at 1920×1080. Billiard TV adds an owner-published HLS source: branded billiards instruction played at 1920×1080 after about 4.8 seconds and sustained the same 15-second sample. FloHockey 24/7 adds its official YouTube player; actual hockey was inspected after preroll advertising and a station break. YouTube Auto selected 480p, while manually selecting 1080p sustained video and audio for 15 seconds. These selected FAST programmes do not provide full NHL or premium tennis coverage.

The candidate catalogue contains **59 entries: 24 configured HLS channels, two official embeds and 33 entries without a supported in-app player**. The current HLS source/stability ceilings are 17 at 1080p, six at 720p (ALEX, hr, Teledeporte, FIFA+, tagesschau24 and NRWision), and QVC at 540p. Both official embeds sustained 1080p through YouTube's quality settings; earlier 480p Auto samples were observations, not source ceilings. A decoded feed can still show a broadcaster rights slate instead of its scheduled programme. Making every listed channel playable remains unfinished.

ProSieben and SAT.1 were also tested through Joyn itself from Spain. ProSieben returned `CODE: GEO_BLOCKED`; SAT.1 produced no video and failed its entitlement request. Joyn permits framing only on Joyn-controlled domains, and ZDF's live page is SAMEORIGIN. Those free consumer websites therefore cannot be embedded into this independent GitHub Pages origin, and their protected playback URLs must not be scraped or relayed.

The selected channel's programme schedule sits directly below its video: the current programme, progress, upcoming shows and Today/Tomorrow/day-after controls. The full Guide route shows now and next across the catalogue. Exact guide mappings cover 54 of the 59 candidate channels; Terra Mater WILD, FIFA+ FAST, Tennis Channel, Billiard TV and FloHockey retain explicit no-listing states until exact guide IDs are verified. Times use the viewer's local timezone, and programme listings never imply playback entitlement.

Real locally stored broadcaster/channel logos cover all 59 candidate catalogue entries, including the two new sports stations. Logos appear consistently on Home, Search, Favourites, Live TV and Guide surfaces, with a readable text fallback for custom streams. Candidate offline shell cache v17 includes those assets and the compact guide snapshot.

The Sports Hub applies one playback rule to every card. Nine candidate cards open screened television inside the PWA; the remaining cards show their exact official-provider, subscription or regional status. Basketball, ice hockey, golf, cricket, rugby, handball, American football and athletics retain dedicated filters; Billiards is added for Billiard TV.

The player prefers HLS.js in Chromium and records signal success only after decoded video time advances. HLS starts at the preferred HD rendition and then remains adaptive, so bandwidth pressure can lower the rendition instead of retaining a permanent manual quality lock. Per-channel ceilings preserve the stable 720p limits for ALEX and NRWision. Auto and explicit manual quality choices remain available.

On 23 October 2026, a fresh audit of the published 57-entry baseline found the broken Tennis CORS path. The repaired candidate's [complete channel audit](CHANNEL_AUDIT_2026-10-23.md), completed at 00:32:59 Europe/Paris, then passed all 59 entry-state checks: 24 HLS transports sustained the ten-second gate, two official embeds advanced non-advertising video, and 33 unsupported entries remained honest. There were zero unstable transports, audit failures or browser errors. Seventeen HLS samples rendered 1080p, six rendered 720p and QVC rendered 540p. Separate visible-picture review found 20 actual HLS programmes and rights notices on hr, BR Nord, BR Süd and ARD-alpha; Das Erste showed its programme. Deterministic checks, 28/28 HLS source paths, the 390×844 mobile nine-card/logo/overflow check and offline v17 shell reload passed. A throttled-network sample confirmed adaptation with a brief rebuffer. Local gates are complete and the pre-release is ready to deploy; GitHub QA, Pages deployment and exact deployed-build verification remain pending. These samples do not guarantee future uptime or programme rights.

Premium RTL/ProSieben/Sat.1, Sky, DAZN, F1, football and similar services remain unavailable in the built-in player unless the rights holder supplies an authorised third-party integration. Joyn, Zattoo, FIFA+ on DAZN and other legitimate platforms were researched individually; an account or free tier is not an embed licence. The app does not scrape protected streams, bypass DRM/geographic controls, or advertise paid channels as free.

Run `node scripts/update-epg.cjs` to refresh the programme snapshot, `node tests/smoke.cjs` for deterministic UI/catalogue checks, `node tests/probe-sources.cjs` for the network-path audit, and `playwright-cli run-code --filename=tests/browser-full-playback-audit.js` from an open Playwright session for the complete candidate catalogue audit. GitHub Actions refreshes the guide twice daily. See `QA_REPORT.md`, `SOURCES.md`, and `CODEX_HANDOFF.md` for evidence and release gates. ## v0.10 — Sports playback and provider directory

The in-app Sports dashboard includes direct, one-click HLS sources for Red Bull TV, More Than Sports TV, Teledeporte (RTVE Spain), Motorvision free FAST and World of Freesports. Four decoded at 1920×1080; Teledeporte decoded at its 1280×720 source maximum. Clicking those cards automatically starts the associated channel inside Global TV Hub. DF1 remains unavailable because its official source blocks Spain.

Official access entries now cover football, Formula 1, tennis, basketball, ice hockey, golf, cricket, rugby, handball, American football, athletics and other Olympic sports. They do not silently redirect and are not mislabelled as working streams. A licensed provider connection is still required before any subscription or provider-controlled event can play inside this independent PWA.

**Important:** These sources received a new short Spain browser and quality screen on 21 October 2026, documented in `SOURCES.md`. That does not guarantee future CORS, worldwide rights, uptime, audio or long-run stability. The player automatically retries configured alternatives and offers quality choices when the manifest advertises them.

**Premium sports:** Sky Sport F1, Sky Sports UK, full Formula 1 DAZN Spain and other DRM-protected paid services have no public licensed embeddable feed here. They remain marked unavailable for in-app playback; their entries must not trigger provider redirects, source-entry prompts, or misrepresent other sports content as premium F1/football.

**QA:** Node smoke tests should verify channel catalogue, one-click sport-card routing, seven navigation routes, private optional custom stream storage, no website redirect, and service worker version bump. GitHub Pages deployment should pass on main.

## v0.7 — Spain source feedback
ARD previously selected its domestic stream before the international source. The player now prioritizes the international feed. A video decoder reporting playback does not mean the programme is not subject to rights restrictions: use Try another feed, or use Report rights slate when the broadcaster replaces the programme picture. The old football screenshot has been removed from the generic player overlay. More free-to-view German channels have been added as public stream candidates; source availability and CORS remain unverified from Spain.
